Our method

Growth happens in stages. We know them all.

In e-commerce, growth never comes in a straight line. It comes in stages — and each stage demands something different. Much of the most important work happens before the results you can see: positioning, margins, a converting site, a unique expression in the market.

Far too many focus only on the visible — products and ads. Our job is to make every stage of your company's growth efficient and position you for success: knowing when to hit the accelerator, and when to hold back.

Stage 01

Foundation

Tracking, site optimisation, margins and offers in place. Often including Shopify optimisation or migration.

Stage 02

Position

Differentiation, core narrative and a creative system that makes your brand impossible to mistake.

Stage 03

Acceleration

Paid advertising scaled in a structured way — with clear targets for CAC, contribution margin and creative renewal.

Stage 04

Scaling

New markets, new products and new channels — when the foundation can carry it, and the data confirms it.

Before you book

What people ask before they say yes.

01What does it cost to work with you?+

It depends on your situation and stage — which is why we always start with a free growth analysis. You get an honest assessment of the potential and a concrete proposal before you decide. We do not work with hourly billing, but with fixed engagement models built around your goals.

02How long is the commitment?+

Growth happens in stages, and the best results come from long-term partnerships. But we do not believe in keeping anyone with contracts — we believe in keeping them with results and honest communication. We go through the details openly in the initial dialogue.

03When do we start seeing results?+

The honest answer: it depends on where your foundation is today. Some things can be felt within the first weeks — others, like positioning and brand building, are built over months. In the growth analysis we tell you concretely what you can realistically expect and when.

04Who will be working on our business?+

No interns, no junior consultants, no sales reps. You work directly with a small, experienced team — and with systems and a data layer built through working with brands across many categories.

05Which channels and platforms do you work with?+

Meta, Google and TikTok are the core channels in paid advertising. Beyond that, Shopify as the platform (including our own app), CRO, creative production, translation for Scandinavian markets and logistics through our own 3PL.

06Do we need to be on Shopify to work with you?+

No — but early in the process we assess whether your current platform can carry your growth ambitions. We see Shopify as the strongest platform for scaling lifestyle brands, and any migration is handled by our own Shopify specialists.
